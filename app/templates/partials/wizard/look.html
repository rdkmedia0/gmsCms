"Take everything" and "just the look" are both answers to "shall I apply this?"; showing the styling options declines to answer yet and goes and shows you, which is what makes this an introduction rather than a gate. A library of ready-made looks comes with this app. Pick one and you get its colours, fonts, layout and — if you want them — its example pages to edit over. You can change any of it afterwards, and switch look again whenever you like.
