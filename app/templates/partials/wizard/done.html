Two things worth knowing before you start

You edit the site on the site. Open your own pages with View Site, and everything on them can be clicked and typed into — headings, text, pictures. There is no separate editor to learn.

The panels down the right-hand edge are where the rest lives: the toolbox adds a tool to a page, the palette changes colours, the ruler changes fonts and corners, and the speech bubble is the assistant.
