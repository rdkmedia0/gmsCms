{# The same fifteen legal_* settings the Legal pages screen writes, asked once here so two features that would otherwise need a separate errand -- the legal pages, and sending a newsletter at all -- come free. #}

Used on your legal pages and at the foot of every newsletter. An email to a list has to carry a real postal address — that is the law in Europe, Switzerland and the US alike, and this site refuses to send one without it.

There are more fields — VAT number, company number, who is responsible — on the Legal pages screen when you need them.
