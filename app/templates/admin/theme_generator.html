2 · Where the words come from

My site’s words means the pages on this site as they read now — your own writing, not the demo content a template came with. Keeping them needs no AI at all and is the quickest way to try a new look on the site you already have; saying them differently keeps every fact and changes only the voice. Writing new words starts from the design concept below instead.

{% include "partials/ai_notice.html" %}

Accepted: Word .docx, plain text .txt, Markdown .md. Anything else is refused — paste it in as text instead.

Real content that has to appear on the site — a CV, a price list, an about page, a menu. Paste it here or open a file below. Every name, date and figure in it is used exactly as written, and nothing is invented around it. Leave it empty and the concept above is written from on its own.

This becomes the template's own content, so anything personal in it travels with the template if you export or share it later.

One per line, the first is the front page. Leave it empty and you get one page called Home.

3 · How it should look

The colours, the typefaces and the shapes are worked out from your description — this app picks from its own controls, so everything it chooses is something you can change afterwards by hand. These are preferences, not requirements: anything you set here wins.

In your own words, or leave it and the description decides.

{# Pictures, not links. There WAS a "paste a link" field here that fetched the page and read its CSS. It went for two reasons an install's owner cares about more than we do: a small site's server reaching out to third-party pages, repeatedly, from one address, is what a scraper looks like -- and being taken for one costs THEM their reachability, not us.
