Sync & keep

Orders here are a cache of Stripe, which holds the full record of every payment. Sync pulls in any checkouts this site missed and keeps this list to a window: a keep-from date below, before which settled orders (nothing left to use) are cleared to stop the table growing forever. They stay in full in Stripe — pull them back any time with an earlier date, e.g. for an audit. An order a buyer can still use (a session or download left) is never cleared, whatever its age. Blank keeps everything.
