Syncing & keeping

Orders are a cache of Stripe, which holds the record of every payment. Sync pulls in any paid checkouts this site missed (a closed tab, or a spell offline). Set a keep-from date and syncing also drops this site’s copy of anything before it — the tidy way to set aside old or test sales. Stripe keeps every payment, and the pull uses the same date, so nothing dropped comes back. Leave the date blank to keep everything.
