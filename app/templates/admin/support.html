The line under your footer

Every site built with gmsCms shows one small line under its footer — “Built with gmsCms, which is free to use. Site owners can remove this line by supporting the project.” — and that is the one thing it asks for. A supporter receives a key that removes it — once, for good. A key does not run out and is not a subscription. It is for one site; a second site is a second key.
