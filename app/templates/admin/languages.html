Languages

Offer your site in other languages. Turn the ones you want on, then press Translate once — your site is translated and stored, so visitors are served their own language instantly. Your business name is never translated. A visitor's language is chosen from their browser, and they can switch it with the Language tool (add it to your header from the Tools menu).
