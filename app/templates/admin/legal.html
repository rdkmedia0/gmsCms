Legal pages

Selling to people in Europe means telling them a few things in writing before they pay: who you are, what happens if they change their mind, and what you do with their details. Answer the questions below and these pages get written for you.

A starting point, not legal advice. The wording follows the European rules that apply to selling at a distance, which is the strictest version most small sellers meet. Read it through and change anything that isn't how you actually work — once written, they're ordinary pages you can edit like any other.
