Products

Everything you sell, managed from here — add it, price it, photograph it, and say what a sale should actually deliver. Changes are pushed straight to Stripe, so there is no second place to keep up to date. Stripe knows what each product costs and takes the money. It has no idea that "10 Coaching Sessions" should become ten bookings someone can claim, or that you only have three of something in a cupboard. Say it here once, and every future sale of that product is handled for you.
