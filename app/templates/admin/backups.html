Backups

A copy of everything: your pages, orders, customers, bookings, uploaded pictures and the files people have paid for. This is the only thing that helps if the site is deleted or damaged — nothing else here recovers from that.

Back up now

Without the key, your saved Stripe and Cal.com credentials can't be read from the backup — on this machine they still work as normal, and restoring somewhere else just means typing those few keys in again. That's on purpose: it means a backup that goes astray can't be used to spend your money.
